Our Story

Rooted in tradition.
Refined by experience.

Botanic Turkish Barbers has been a fixture of Belfast's Botanic Quarter since 2014. Founded by master barber Mehmet Yilmaz, the shop was built on a simple belief — that a truly great cut is the result of patience, skill, and genuine care for the client.

We practice techniques passed down through generations of Turkish barbering: the hot towel ritual, open-blade shaping, and the unhurried attention that turns a haircut into an experience. Every man who sits in our chair leaves looking — and feeling — like himself, only sharper.

Meet the Barbers

The hands behind the craft.

Each of our barbers brings years of dedicated training and a genuine passion for their work.

Mehmet Yilmaz

Master Barber · Founder

Trained in Istanbul and Belfast, Mehmet brings 18 years of mastery to every cut. He founded Botanic Turkish Barbers with a vision of bringing authentic Turkish craftsmanship to Northern Ireland.

Ali Demir

Senior Barber

Ali's razor-sharp precision and easy manner have made him a favourite among regulars. Specialises in fades, skin fades, and classic gentlemen's cuts with a contemporary edge.

Connor Walsh

Barber

Belfast-born and Botanic-trained, Connor has a natural talent for textured cuts and beard sculpting. His calm chair-side manner puts first-timers immediately at ease.

Karim Hassan

Barber

Karim joined the team in 2021 after completing his training in London. His bold creativity and technical precision have quickly made him a trusted name among younger clientele.
